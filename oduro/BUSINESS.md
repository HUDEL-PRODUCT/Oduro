# Oduro

Business notes. Updated 3 October 2026.

Oduro is the public name of Oduro Enterprises, the Companies House company. No social handles are claimed yet. The website hosts the full catalog below. There is no flagship package: the enquiry form routes each person, and the catalog is what gets delivered.

Oduro designs, builds, and improves the infrastructure behind customer acquisition and delivery. We connect the tools, teams, and information a business depends on.

Client work is built inside accounts the client owns and pays for. They keep the system if the retainer ends. The Oduro site is the exception: Oduro Enterprises is the client, so those accounts stay with Oduro.

Stack and the dogfood enquiry flow are in [STACK.md](STACK.md).

## Positioning

Sell one outcome. Keep the buyer broad for now; niche can tighten later.

**Outcome.** A connected business — growth systems, automation, software, and AI designed to work as one. Oduro designs, builds, and improves the infrastructure behind customer acquisition and delivery by connecting the tools, teams, and information the business depends on.

**Buyer.** Broad for v1. Homepage leads with interconnection, not a named niche. Rough size still useful later: businesses roughly 5–50 people who feel the cost of disconnected tools and missed follow-up, and can pay for a setup plus a retainer. Niche candidates when we narrow: professional services, clinics and appointment businesses, home-service and trade companies with a real website and a real admin bottleneck.

**How the catalog fits.** The four pillars are the parts we install. The homepage sells the connected-business outcome. The catalog lets a visitor see the full range and open any solution in depth before they enquire.

**First system (dogfood).** The Oduro site runs the first system we install for ourselves: enquire → instant tailored email → book a conversation → one nurture if they have not booked. Orchestration runs in **Make** (client-owned org; Oduro Enterprises for the dogfood site). That loop is proof of connection around acquisition, not the whole brand promise.

**Design reference.** [Agentryx](https://www.agentryx.io) is a parallel brand used only for colour feel and information-depth inspiration. Do not clone copy or position Oduro as its successor on the site.

Custom software stays on the site. It is quoted when a workflow cannot live inside the tools already in place, usually after a smaller system is already running.

- Also store **which pillar and which solution** they came from (attribution), even when they change the primary route. Use that note to tailor follow-up communications (instant email and later nurture).
- Form fields and the dogfood send/nurture rules stay in [STACK.md](STACK.md).

## Every project

Every project runs the same way.

1. **Audit.** Map how acquisition, delivery, data, and follow-up work today. Name what is disconnected or leaking and what we will not touch.
2. **Design.** Agree the system, the tools, the content the AI may use, and what “done” means before build starts.
3. **Build.** Install the agreed system in accounts the client owns. Use a tool they already pay for when it can do the job. When it cannot, they open the new account and invite Oduro in.
4. **Test.** Run the unhappy paths: bad data, duplicates, after-hours, opt-out, and what the assistant says when it does not know.
5. **Launch.** Hand over a system the client can run, with a record of what was installed and what they own.
6. **Improve.** Watch it in a retainer: fix what breaks, tighten copy and flows, add the next piece from the catalog.
